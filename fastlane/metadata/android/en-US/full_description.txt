Reliquary is an Android app for <a href="https://www.tubearchivist.com">Tube Archivist</a>, the self-hosted YouTube archive. It needs your own Tube Archivist server, version 0.6.0 or later.

<b>Watch offline</b>
<ul>
<li>Download single videos, or keep a channel or playlist downloaded automatically, with optional Wi-Fi-only downloads.</li>
<li>Downloads resume after interruptions and include thumbnails and subtitles.</li>
<li>Offline, the app shows what's on the device and keeps working.</li>
</ul>

<b>Progress that syncs</b>
<ul>
<li>Your place in each video is saved on the device and sent to your server when it can be reached, so you can carry on in the web player.</li>
</ul>

<b>Player</b>
<ul>
<li>Resumes where you left off and plays playlists as a queue.</li>
<li>Minimises into a mini-player, plays in the background and in picture-in-picture.</li>
<li>Subtitles, SponsorBlock skipping, and the comments and similar videos your server saved.</li>
</ul>

Reliquary only talks to your Tube Archivist server. It has no tracking or ads, and doesn't contact YouTube.
